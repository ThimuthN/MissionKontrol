# Workflow evidence

**Case**
One instance of work, such as a single laptop request or the screening of one candidate for one role.

**Stage**
A business checkpoint that a reviewer can inspect. “Interview scheduled” and “interview attended” describe different checkpoints.

**Evidence link**
A proposed association between a source record and a stage in a case. Its reason explains why the record belongs there.

**Observed**
A record directly supports the stated checkpoint. A calendar entry can directly support a booking.

**Inferred**
A record supports a checkpoint through an indirect match, such as a candidate alias combined with a mailbox and a nearby appointment.

**Missing**
The case has no linked record supporting that checkpoint. The real-world outcome remains open.

**Correction**
A reviewer’s dated decision to exclude or restore a particular evidence link. The original record and matching reason remain available for inspection.
